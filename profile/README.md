# CTP Space Systems

Tesla predicted. Drazen discovered. Resofactor continues.

CTP Space Systems is the spacecraft architecture and gravitic propulsion division of CTP, founded in 2022. The next SpaceX, without the rockets: exploration and deep space rather than defense. The craft come from the science, not from an iteration on chemical launch.

CTP is Conscious Translational Physics, the science the late Drazen Premate discovered and the body of work Resofactor carries forward as heir and successor to Nikola Tesla. It describes two energy units, C and T, and the processes that render physical reality, which is why consciousness, matter, time, gravity and propulsion all sit downstream of them.

The site lives at [ctp-space-systems.github.io](https://ctp-space-systems.github.io/)

## The craft

The propulsion is gravitic, not chemical. The craft are built from the CTP processes that render physical reality, which is why they do not need rockets to leave the planet or to travel between the stars. The vision is interstellar travel within 10 to 15 years to one generation, whichever comes first.

## Inside the CTPVerse

CTP Space Systems sits inside the CTPVerse with the rest of the ecosystem: CTP Energy and the science, CTP-EOS and the software group, CTP Music, CTP Magazine, FIN-DSP, CTP University, and the consumer, wellness and infrastructure units. It is not a separate company with a loose association. It is one science with several applications, each one a market the science opens rather than a business that happens to be nearby.

Capital first, research second, craft third. Capital funds operations now and builds the facility; research happens inside that facility and nowhere else; craft follows research.
